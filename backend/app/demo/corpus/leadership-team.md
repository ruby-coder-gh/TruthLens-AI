# Northwind Renewables — Leadership Team

Northwind Renewables is led by an executive team with deep experience across
renewable energy development, project finance, and grid operations. This page is
maintained by the corporate communications team and reflects the leadership
structure as of the most recent company-wide town hall.

## Executive Committee

**Dana Whitfield — Chief Executive Officer**
Dana Whitfield became Chief Executive Officer in January 2022. She succeeded
founder Erik Solberg, who moved to a non-executive chairman role. Before joining
Northwind Renewables, Dana spent eleven years at a Nordic offshore wind developer,
most recently as Chief Operating Officer, where she oversaw the construction of two
offshore wind farms totalling 900 MW. Dana holds an MSc in Mechanical Engineering
from the Technical University of Denmark and serves on the board of a regional
grid-operator advisory panel.

**Marcus Oyelaran — Chief Financial Officer**
Marcus joined in 2020 from an infrastructure investment fund, where he led over
€2 billion of renewable energy transactions. He is responsible for the Company's
financing strategy, including its green bond programme and the project financing
structure underpinning the Aurora offshore wind project.

**Priya Nair — Chief Operating Officer**
Priya leads asset operations across the Company's onshore wind, offshore wind, and
solar portfolio. She joined in 2019 as Head of Onshore Operations and was promoted
to Chief Operating Officer in 2023. Priya previously worked in turbine reliability
engineering for a major original equipment manufacturer.

**Tomas Vávra — Chief Development Officer**
Tomas leads project development, including the Aurora offshore project and the
twelve-project onshore and solar pipeline described in the Company's project
pipeline disclosure. He joined in 2021 and previously led offshore development for
a competing developer in the same core market.

**Helena Brandt — General Counsel**
Helena oversees legal, regulatory, and permitting matters group-wide, including the
permitting strategy for Aurora and the Fjellheim repowering programme. She joined
in 2018.

## Board of Directors

The board comprises seven directors, five of whom are independent non-executive
directors. Erik Solberg, the Company's founder, serves as non-executive Chairman.
The board's committees include Audit, Remuneration, and Sustainability, each chaired
by an independent director.

## Reporting Lines

The Chief Operating Officer and Chief Development Officer both report directly to
the Chief Executive Officer. Regional operations leads for the Company's nine
operating countries report to the Chief Operating Officer. The Aurora project
director reports jointly to the Chief Development Officer and Chief Financial
Officer given the project's scale relative to the Company's existing asset base.

This page is updated after each material change to the executive committee and is
reviewed quarterly by corporate communications for accuracy.
