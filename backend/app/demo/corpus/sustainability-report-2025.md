# Northwind Renewables — 2025 Sustainability Report

## Overview

This report summarises Northwind Renewables' environmental, social, and governance
performance for the 2025 calendar year. It is intended to be read alongside the
Company's Annual Report and covers the same consolidated group of onshore wind,
offshore wind, and solar assets across nine European countries.

## Environmental Performance

### Emissions Intensity

The single metric our board and investors track most closely is the carbon
emissions intensity of the electricity we generate, measured in grams of CO2
equivalent per kilowatt-hour and indexed against our 2020 baseline year. Emissions
intensity was 41% below the 2020 baseline. This was the largest single-year
improvement since we began reporting this metric in 2019.

This improvement was driven by three factors. First, the full-year contribution of
the Kestrel Ridge onshore expansion displaced generation that had previously come
from a gas-backed balancing arrangement. Second, we renegotiated two legacy biomass
co-firing contracts at our Solheim site, reducing the associated emissions factor by
roughly a third. Third, transmission losses across our internal grid connections
improved following the completion of a substation upgrade programme at three sites.

We have restated our methodology this year to more precisely allocate embodied
emissions from turbine and panel manufacturing across each asset's operating life,
which affects the comparability of this figure with prior-year disclosures; the
prior-year figure under the restated methodology is included in the appendix.

### Water and Land Use

Water withdrawal per MWh generated fell 9% year over year, driven by improved
cooling-water recycling at our two thermal balancing plants (both scheduled for
retirement by 2028). Land under active habitat restoration across our onshore sites
grew to 340 hectares, up from 265 hectares in 2024, as three additional sites
completed their post-construction restoration programmes.

### Environmental Incidents

We recorded zero reportable environmental incidents in 2025, extending our streak
to three consecutive years. All sites maintained ISO 14001 certification throughout
the year.

## Social Performance

### Workforce

Northwind Renewables employed **1,240 people** at year end, an increase of 94 from
2024. Women represented 34% of the workforce and 28% of engineering roles, both up
roughly two percentage points from the prior year. We continued our apprenticeship
programme in partnership with technical colleges in three of our operating markets,
graduating 47 apprentices in 2025.

### Health and Safety

Our total recordable incident rate improved to 1.8 per 200,000 hours worked, from
2.3 in 2024, reflecting the safety programme introduced across all construction
sites in mid-2024. We had zero fatalities for the sixth consecutive year across our
own workforce and contractors.

### Community Engagement

We disbursed €4.2 million through community benefit funds tied to our onshore wind
sites, funding 61 local projects ranging from school renewable-energy education
programmes to habitat restoration grants.

## Governance

The board's Sustainability Committee met four times in 2025 and reviewed emissions
performance, the supplier code of conduct rollout, and progress against our 2030
science-based targets at each meeting. All turbine and balance-of-plant suppliers
with annual spend above €5 million are now required to report Scope 1 and 2
emissions, with Scope 3 reporting phasing in from 2026.

## Looking Ahead

We expect emissions intensity improvements to moderate in 2026 and 2027 as the
easiest efficiency gains are exhausted, before improving again once Aurora begins
displacing older balancing capacity from 2027 onward. We remain committed to
publishing this report annually and to having our emissions data independently
assured, as we have done since 2022.
