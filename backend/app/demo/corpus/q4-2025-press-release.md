# Northwind Renewables Reports Fourth-Quarter and Full-Year 2025 Results

**AMSTERDAM — 12 February 2026** — Northwind Renewables N.V. (the "Company") today
reported financial results for the fourth quarter and full year ended 31 December
2025.

## Full-Year 2025 Highlights

- Full-year revenue of **€398 million**, compared with €356 million in 2024
- Total installed generating capacity of **1.8 GW** across onshore wind, offshore
  wind, and solar assets in nine countries
- Fourth-quarter revenue of €108 million, in line with internal guidance
- Continued construction progress on the Aurora offshore wind project, with
  commercial commissioning still targeted for **Q3 2027**

"2025 was a year of steady execution," said Dana Whitfield, Chief Executive Officer.
"Our existing fleet performed well, our balance sheet remains strong, and Aurora is
tracking to plan. We enter 2026 with good visibility into both near-term cash flow
and the growth Aurora will bring from 2027 onward."

## Segment Performance

Revenue in 2025 was €398 million. Onshore wind generation was broadly flat year over year on a same-asset basis, with
growth coming entirely from the Kestrel Ridge expansion, which contributed a full
twelve months of output in 2025 versus roughly nine months in 2024. Solar generation
benefited from favourable irradiance in the second and third quarters and higher
average merchant prices in the Company's Nordic portfolio. Offshore wind generation
from the existing Meridian array was in line with the prior year.

Full-year EBITDA margin was consistent with the Company's target range in the
mid-40s percent, reflecting disciplined operating cost control across the fleet even
as development spend increased ahead of Aurora's construction phase.

## Fourth-Quarter Detail

Fourth-quarter revenue of €108 million reflected typical seasonal wind resource
patterns in the Company's core markets. The quarter included the start of turbine
foundation fabrication for Aurora at the Company's contracted supplier's fabrication
yard, and the substantial completion of onshore substation groundworks at the Aurora
landfall site.

## Aurora Project Update

Aurora is expected to commission in the third quarter of 2027. The project remains
600 MW of contracted capacity backed by a 20-year power purchase agreement.
Management noted no material change to the project's cost or schedule baseline
during the quarter and reaffirmed prior capital expenditure guidance for the
project.

## 2026 Guidance

The Company reiterated that it expects full-year 2026 capital expenditure of
€640–680 million, weighted toward Aurora construction and the ongoing Fjellheim
repowering programme. Management will provide additional detail on 2026 guidance
during its full-year results call.

## About Northwind Renewables

Northwind Renewables N.V. develops, owns, and operates onshore wind, offshore wind,
and solar generating assets across Northern and Western Europe. The Company's shares
trade on Euronext Amsterdam.
